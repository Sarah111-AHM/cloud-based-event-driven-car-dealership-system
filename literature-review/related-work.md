
# Literature Review

Several studies have looked at cloud-based and event-driven systems for businesses and dealerships. This review summarizes key findings that relate to our project.

1. **Cloud-Based Inventory Management:**  
   - Many studies show that storing inventory in the cloud improves accessibility, reduces errors, and allows multiple branches to work together in real time.  

2. **Event-Driven Architecture in Retail:**  
   - Research indicates that using events to trigger automatic actions (like sales updates or stock changes) increases system efficiency and reduces delays.  

3. **Performance and Scalability Analysis:**  
   - Several papers analyzed cloud systems under heavy workloads and concluded that cloud architecture allows better scaling and cost efficiency compared to traditional systems.  

4. **Integrated Management Systems:**  
   - Combining cloud storage, event-driven workflows, and analytics provides a more complete solution for managing operations in dealerships or retail chains.  

**Conclusion:**  
Our project builds on these ideas by designing a **cloud-based, event-driven system** specifically for car dealerships. It applies lessons from previous studies and evaluates **performance, scalability, and cost efficiency** in a structured way.
