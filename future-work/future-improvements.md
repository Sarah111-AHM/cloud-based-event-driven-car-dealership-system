# Future Work and Improvements

This section discusses possible future improvements for the **cloud-based, event-driven car dealership system**.

## 1. Integration with AI

- Add **AI-powered recommendations** for customers, like suggesting cars based on preferences and past purchases.  
- Predict **sales trends** using machine learning to help managers make better decisions.  

## 2. Mobile Application

- Develop a **mobile app** for sales staff and customers.  
- Allow real-time updates on sales, inventory, and customer requests from smartphones or tablets.  

## 3. Advanced Analytics

- Include **dashboards with predictive analytics** for inventory management.  
- Track **customer behavior** to improve marketing and sales strategies.  

## 4. Blockchain for Security

- Store sensitive data such as customer information, invoices, and contracts on a **blockchain ledger**.  
- Ensure **immutability and data integrity** for all transactions.  

## 5. Multi-Region Support

- Expand the system to support **dealerships in multiple cities or countries**.  
- Ensure **low-latency access** for branches in different regions.  

---

## Conclusion

The proposed future work shows how the system can evolve into a **smarter, more secure, and globally scalable platform**. These improvements will further enhance efficiency, accuracy, and customer satisfaction in car dealership operations.
