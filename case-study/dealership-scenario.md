# Case Study: BMW Car Sale Workflow

This case study demonstrates how the **cloud-based, event-driven system** works in a real BMW dealership scenario.

## Scenario

- **Branch:** BMW Main City Dealership  
- **Event:** Customer buys a car (BMW X5 2026)  
- **Users Involved:** Salesperson, Inventory Manager, Accounting Staff  

---

## Workflow Steps

1. **Sale Event Triggered:**  
   - The salesperson enters the sale details in the system.  
   - This action generates a **"Car Sold" event**.  

2. **Inventory Update:**  
   - The system automatically reduces the number of available BMW X5 cars in stock.  
   - Alerts are sent if stock is low.  

3. **Customer Record Update:**  
   - Customer information is stored in the cloud.  
   - Previous purchases, preferences, and financing details are updated.  

4. **Invoice Generation:**  
   - A digital invoice is automatically created and emailed to the customer.  

5. **Dashboard Update:**  
   - Sales metrics, inventory levels, and branch performance are updated in **real time**.  

---

## Benefits Observed

- **Real-Time Processing:** All actions happen instantly without delay.  
- **Error Reduction:** Automated updates reduce manual mistakes.  
- **Scalability:** Adding more sales events or branches does not slow the system.  
- **Cost Efficiency:** Less staff time needed for manual updates.  

---

## Conclusion

This BMW case study shows how **event-driven workflows in a cloud system** make dealership operations faster, accurate, and scalable. It confirms the effectiveness of our proposed design for **luxury car dealerships**.
